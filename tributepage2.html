The Man Behind the Armor

Tony Stark was a brilliant inventor and the CEO of Stark Industries. Known for his extraordinary intelligence, confidence, and unconventional personality, he transformed himself from a weapons manufacturer into one of Earth's greatest defenders.

After being captured and witnessing the consequences of his own weapons, Tony built the first Iron Man armor and began a journey that would change his life forever.
