Breaking News: Student Opens Textbook Five Minutes Before Exam
Experts describe the situation as “extremely dangerous.”
By Your Name

A student shocked classmates today after opening a textbook just five minutes before an important examination. Witnesses say the student stared at the first page for several seconds before realizing that the book contained approximately 300 pages of information.

Despite the difficult circumstances, the student remained confident and claimed that everything would be fine. When asked what they had studied, the student reportedly replied, “I watched a video about it once.” Teachers have advised students not to repeat the strategy.
